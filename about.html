Editorial Transparency & Verification Standards

How WARDOGS HQ produces fact-grounded, non-hallucinated guides.

The Fact-Checking Protocol

Every guide, tier list, and configuration table published on this platform undergoes triangular verification:

1

Steam Official Telemetry

Minimum/recommended system specifications, patch notes, and engine metadata are retrieved directly from official developer releases.

2

In-Game High-Definition Keyframe Extraction

Visual assets are harvested from real 4K gameplay footage and passed through 5-dimensional mathematical visual evaluation (Laplacian variance > 100, RMS contrast, pHash deduplication).
