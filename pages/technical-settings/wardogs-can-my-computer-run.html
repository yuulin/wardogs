WARDOGS System Requirements: Can Your PC Run It? (Official Specs With Framerate Targets)

Author: WARDOGS Editorial Team • Category: Technical & Optimization • Tier: FACTOID • Last Updated: August 2026
⚡ Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF): WARDOGS needs a GTX 1660 / RX 590 with an i5-8600 or Ryzen 5 3500 just to hit the official minimum — which is specced at 1080p Low @ 60fps upscaled. The recommended tier (RTX 3070 / RX 6700 XT with an i7-12700K) targets 1440p medium @ 70fps+ native or 4K medium @ 60fps+ upscaled. Both tiers demand 16 GB RAM, 50 GB storage, and broadband internet — this is always-online multiplayer warfare.

Official Minimum Requirements — With a Real Framerate Promise

Unlike most store-page fine print, Bulkhead's Steam page attaches explicit performance contracts to each tier:

  • OS: Windows 10 (64-bit)
  • CPU: Intel Core i5-8600 or AMD Ryzen 5 3500
  • RAM: 16 GB
  • GPU: NVIDIA GTX 1660 or Radeon RX 590
  • Network: Broadband internet connection (mandatory)
  • Storage: 50 GB available space
  • Target: 1080p Low @ 60fps (Upscaled)

Official Recommended Requirements — 1440p Native Territory

  • OS: Windows 11
  • CPU: Intel Core i7-12700K or AMD Ryzen 7 5700X
  • RAM: 16 GB
  • GPU: NVIDIA RTX 3070 or AMD RX 6700 XT
  • Network: Broadband connection
  • Storage: 50 GB available space
  • Target: 1440p Medium @ 70fps+ (Native), or 4K Medium @ 60fps+ (Upscaled)

Publishing upscaling as part of BOTH tiers signals the engine leans on reconstruction tech for its 100-player battlefields — and that Bulkhead intends you to know exactly what performance you are buying.

Why This Shooter's Spec Sheet Reads Higher Than Usual

A 100-player, three-faction battlefield with destructible environments has to simulate and serialize far more actors than a typical 64-player shooter. The GTX 1660 baseline (rather than the classic 1060-tier entry point we see on smaller-scale titles) reflects that simulation load. The other tell: broadband is listed as a hard requirement line item, not a footnote — matches are persistent online warfare first, everything else second, per the Multi-player / Online PvP category set.

Planning Your Upgrade Before September 10

The VRAM jump between tiers (GTX 1660-class 6 GB → RTX 3070-class 8 GB) plus the CPU step from a locked 6-core i5 to a 12700K tells you where money moves the needle: GPU first, single-thread/CPU generation second, RAM not at all (16 GB covers both rows). Closed beta veterans already ran this software at scale — over 100,000 players participated in the August 21–23 closed beta according to Games.gg's launch coverage, so community-reported performance threads are worth mining for your specific GPU family.
