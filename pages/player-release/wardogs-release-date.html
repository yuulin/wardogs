WARDOGS Release Date & Early Access Roadmap: Everything Officially Confirmed

Author: WARDOGS Editorial Team • Category: Live Stats & Roadmap • Tier: FACTOID • Last Updated: August 2026
⚡ Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF): WARDOGS enters Steam Early Access on September 10, 2026 at $39.99 — PC-exclusive at launch, with console releases planned afterward. Bulkhead frames EA as a 1–2 year program; over 100,000 players already tested the build during the August 21–23 closed beta.

The Date, Platform Reality & Price — Verbatim From Coverage

Polygon confirms the September 10, 2026 Early Access date alongside its $39.99 price point. Platform scope matters equally: EA launches exclusively on Steam, with GamesRadar noting planned Xbox and PS5 releases further out. Console hopefuls should set calendar reminders rather than storefront wishlists for now.

Beta Recap: The Numbers That Already Happened

The closed beta ran August 21–23, guaranteed for preorder buyers, and Games.gg reports 100,000+ participants flooding in — momentum strong enough that preorder surge coverage tracked it atop Steam's premium sellers chart. Earlier milestones add context: the project crossed its million-wishlist mark publicly acknowledged via official Steam news communications back in August per our prior tracking.

Early Access Duration: Bulkhead's Own Numbers

The Steam Early Access FAQ quotes the studio directly: roughly 1 to 2 years in EA, framed around adamant refusal to rush development — content cadence, economy tuning, and balance passes follow community feedback loops typical of long-form MP programs. Translation for planners: expect quarterly-ish content beats and frequent balance churn across the tenure rather than a quick 1.0 sprint.

Roadmap Signals vs Silence

No dated public roadmap document enumerates season content yet. What stands confirmed: destructible-battlefield focus expanding, three-faction warfare deepening, and economy systems iterating through EA. Patch-note velocity becomes the honest signal once Septembers unlocks — we track every official update as it lands, replacing projections with receipts.
