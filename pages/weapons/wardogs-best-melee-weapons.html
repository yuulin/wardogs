4. Five Fatal Combat Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

Over 80% of player deaths in WARDOGS stem from predictable mechanical misjudgments. Review these critical pitfalls to protect your progression:

⚠️

1. Depleting the Stamina Bar to Absolute Zero

Emptying stamina induces a 1.2-second heavy breathing penalty during which you cannot sprint, dodge, or block. Always reserve 20-25% stamina for emergency evasions.

⚠️

2. Over-Committing to Full 4-Hit Attack Combos

The final swing in standard weapon combos carries an extended recovery lock. Stop at 2-3 hits unless the target is visibly staggered or isolated.

⚠️

3. Fighting Multiple Enemies on Open Ground

AI units coordinate pincer attacks when surrounding players. Relocate immediately to narrow corridors, door frames, or cliff ledges to force 1v1 choke engagements.

⚠️

4. Neglecting Weapon Sharpness & Edge Degradation

Blunt weapons suffer a 40% damage penalty and bounce off armored targets, creating self-stagger. Apply whetstones regularly before entering high-danger sectors.

⚠️

5. Panic-Rolling Backwards from Heavy Overhead Strikes

Many boss attacks feature tracking hitboxes calibrated to catch backward rolls. Roll forward-diagonally through the attack swing to land safely behind the boss's flank.
